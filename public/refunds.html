No active transactions: No paid orders or subscriptions are offered through this public site. The policy below is being prepared for a future commercial launch and does not apply to a currently purchasable product. Final binding policy details will be published before customer payments begin.

Proposed cancellation approach

Initial access is intended to be sold in individually purchased monthly periods rather than automatic recurring debits. A purchased period would expire unless a new purchase is explicitly made. Any later automatic renewals would require a separate, clear customer authorization and an option to cancel future renewals.

Proposed refund eligibility

  • Duplicate charges or unsuccessful activation: Qualifying erroneous charges or paid access that cannot be activated would be eligible for a full refund after transaction verification.
  • Unused access: A first-purchase refund request within seven calendar days of activation, where no research task was successfully completed and no monitoring job was activated, is the proposed customer-friendly policy.
  • Material service non-delivery: A proven failure to deliver paid access may warrant an appropriate full or proportionate refund, depending on what was provided.
  • After significant use: Change-of-mind requests may be reviewed case by case. Billing errors, defective services and applicable statutory customer rights would still be considered.
