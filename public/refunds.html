Cancellation & Refund Policy — Proposed for Launch

Private pilot: There are currently no public paid subscriptions or orders, so no public payments, cancellations or refunds can be initiated through this preview. The following is a draft policy for review before paid access is enabled; it is not currently an operational paid-service contract.

Planned cancellation terms

For the initial launch, we intend to offer manually renewed monthly access without automatic charges. A paid period would end at the end of its purchased term unless the customer explicitly makes another payment. Any future auto-renewing subscription would require separate authorization and a straightforward option to cancel future renewals. Cancellation would not erase reports already delivered or remove access during a paid period except when required for security or misuse.

Planned refund eligibility

  • Duplicate payment or charged without activation: after checking the transaction, qualifying erroneous charges or a service that cannot be activated would receive a full refund.
  • Changed your mind before use: a request received within 7 calendar days of first activation, with no successfully completed research job and no activated monitoring job, would ordinarily qualify for a full refund.
  • Material failure to deliver the paid service: customers could seek a fair full or proportionate refund based on the unprovided service, rather than losing payment for unavailable service.
  • After substantive use: a change-of-mind refund would not be automatic, but billing errors, non-delivery, defects and rights under applicable consumer law would still be reviewed.

How to ask

Customers would write to razam9281@gmail.com with the order/payment reference and issue description, without sharing passwords, OTPs, full payment card details or government identity numbers. Approved refunds would be initiated to the original payment method, with final credit timing dependent on banks and payment networks.

These proposed terms remain subject to merchant confirmation, operational verification, and applicable law. Statutory consumer rights are not restricted by this draft. For the real operating policy, this page will be updated before any payment checkout goes live.
