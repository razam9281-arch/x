About SHADDOWWATCH

Building a more
aware workflow.

SHADDOWWATCH is a research and monitoring intelligence software project being developed in India. Its aim is to help users investigate complex information and notice relevant changes.

01

Why it exists

Important information is often scattered across websites, documents and updates. Research takes time; changes can be difficult to notice. We are building a workflow to bring both activities together.

02

Where things stand

The platform is in private development. Public research submission, continuous monitoring, subscription activation and payment checkout have not been enabled on this informational website.
