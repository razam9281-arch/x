Usage model

What are AI Tokens?

SHADDOWWATCH AI Tokens are the customer-facing name for actual language-model token usage, not a separate virtual currency. A plan's monthly allocation is shared across eligible research, comparison, discovery and monitoring work. A paid account would display its remaining balance once metering is implemented and verified.

01

One shared balance

The same monthly AI Token allowance applies across eligible features rather than separate independent quotas.

02

Usage-based metering

Input and output language-model tokens would count against the shared balance. Provider-specific categories and counting rules must be documented and tested before activation.

03

Clear plan terms

Final product access, quota behavior and applicable taxes will be disclosed before an account is charged.
